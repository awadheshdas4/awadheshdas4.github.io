About

I am an experimental physicist working in condensed matter physics. I use nonlinear optical and ultrafast spectroscopies to study electronic order, collective phenomena, and light–matter interactions in quantum materials.

Research

  • Nonlinear optical spectroscopy Probing symmetry, electronic order, and hidden phases through the nonlinear optical response.
  • Ultrafast pump-probe spectroscopy Tracking non-equilibrium dynamics and collective excitations on femtosecond to picosecond timescales.
  • THz emission spectroscopy Measuring ultrafast charge, spin, and lattice responses through emitted terahertz radiation.
  • Quantum materials Correlated, topological, and low-dimensional systems where electronic order emerges from collective behavior.
